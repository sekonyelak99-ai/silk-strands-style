# Silk Strands, Noir & Necter Beauty Collective

Create a professional, user-friendly e-commerce store that sells beauty products and hair specifically for Black women. The store must be optimized for conversions and include the following features:

Order Management & Notifications

Customers can track their orders in real time.

Smart email generator automatically sends order confirmations, shipping updates, and delivery notifications.

Customer Support

Integrated virtual assistant/chatbot for troubleshooting and customer inquiries.

AI-Powered Search

AI search assistant to help customers quickly find specific products.

Shopping Cart & Wishlist

Customers can add/remove items from their cart.

Option to save items for later purchase (like a Wishlist).

Payments & Currency Options

Easy-to-use online payment process.

Built-in currency converter so customers can choose their preferred currency before checkout.

Personalized Accounts

Secure sign-in with email and password.

Personalized profiles to enhance shopping experience.

Ability to share purchases as links with others (similar to SHEIN), allowing friends/family to check out or pay on their behalf.

Reviews & Community

Customers can leave product reviews after purchase.

Reviews are visible to help new customers make informed decisions.

Design Requirements:

Professional and modern layout tailored to beauty and hair products.

Mobile-friendly and responsive design.

Optimized for conversions with clear CTAs, easy navigation, and fast checkout.

Make sure the interface has luxurious finishes and neutral colors, as well as a nice font for the name.
